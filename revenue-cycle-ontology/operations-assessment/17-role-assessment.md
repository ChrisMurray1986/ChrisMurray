## Using the role layer

1. **In interviews:** RFMs are hypothesis lists — listen for the patterns, then verify in the
   work (sampled accounts, observed sessions), never accuse from the table.
2. **In reconciliation:** a PFM observed in a process usually has an RFM (behavioral) and an
   OFM (systemic) behind it. The fix differs by layer: coaching/competency (RFM), standard
   work/tooling (PFM), structure/incentives (OFM). Assessments that prescribe only one layer
   relapse.
3. **With the AI stack:** RFMs define what the HITL workforce must be *selected and trained
   for* — the reviewer roles inherit the judgment surfaces listed here, and the seeded-error
   QA (RBP-BOT-03) exists because RFM patterns don't disappear when the work becomes review.
