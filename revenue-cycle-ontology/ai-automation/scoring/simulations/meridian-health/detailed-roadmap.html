Meridian Health — Two-Year AI & Automation Roadmap

Operator-ready plan: every workstream with owner, budget, dependencies, and a falsifiable exit criterion. Generated from the assessment + optimizer output (roadmap.json), phased by the investment-loop playbook (file 11), implemented per the Epic-shop patterns (file 14). Budget $2.5M/quarter; 8 quarters; 70 use-case launches; exit run-rate $69.1M/yr net.

0 · Operating model (stand up in the first 30 days — Playbook Play 0)

  • Loop operator (named program lead, 1.0 FTE): runs the tools, owns the YAML files, produces the quarterly packet. The plan dies without this role.
  • Automation council (CFO sponsor + VP Rev Cycle + CMO/UM + Compliance + CIO delegate + Finance partner): 2-hour quarterly decision meeting; packet pre-read mandatory.
  • Governance body (council subcommittee): risk tiering, autonomy promotions/demotions.
  • Benefit owners: the operational director of each area signs realized value quarterly — never the automation team.
  • Hard rules adopted day one: baseline before every go-live; every launch starts in recommend mode (A1); a remediation is done only when its facet score moves; one benefit ledger, one dollar counted once.

5 · Vendor strategy (runs across all phases)

  • Keep and expand both vendors. The build-vs-buy math confirms the original decisions; expansions (Q6–Q7) are priced at ~20–30% of greenfield build.
  • Q1 renewals agenda: data-rights clauses (Meridian's data does not train vendor models without consent), telemetry access (vendor decisions feed Meridian's monitoring), exit provisions priced. Criteria/reference license amendments negotiated in the same motion.
  • Vendor claims discipline: any vendor "fine-tuned on 10 years of data" claim is evaluated on Meridian's golden sets, blinded, against the internal baseline — never on the pitch (file 12 protocol).
  • Oversight: from Q5, UC-14-07 scores both vendors on the same SLA/telemetry standard as internal automations.

6 · Measurement & governance cadence

  • Weekly: delivery standups; exception-queue and fleet health (from the fabric telemetry).
  • Monthly: run-rate review — HITL hours, token/vendor invoices vs plan; ±20% drift flags early.
  • Quarterly: re-assessment (facet owners, evidence rule) → regenerate packet → council: true-up first, readiness velocity, remediation funding, launches, autonomy promotions, kills. The packet's quarter-over-quarter delta is the program status report.
  • Value integrity: benefit ledger with single counting; value net of run cost; one-time AR release counted once; risk EV segregated. Realized-vs-estimated variance >30% for two quarters recalibrates the driver files.
