DOCTRINE D3 — "ASYMMETRIC-FRICTION"
Wargame 2.0 Adjudication Physics (Payer=Red, Provider=Blue, 2026–2030, quarterly turns)

1. CORE TEMPO RULES

1.1 Deployment clocks. Red capability plays (new edit engines, pend logic, downcode models, prior-auth retraining) activate T+1 by default; T+2 if the play requires state filing or network-contract amendment. Blue capability plays (denial-prediction, autonomous appeals, documentation AI, revenue-integrity tooling) enter a BUILD TRACK of 4–8 turns: roll d6 + capital modifier + governance modifier at play declaration; result sets the base clock (4 turns floor for AMC/large-system archetypes, 6 for regional, 8 for safety-net/rural).

1.2 Terrain rotation vs. re-instrumentation. Each turn, Red may rotate one yield vector (shift denial mix among edits/pends/downcodes, retarget CPT clusters, move friction upstream to eligibility). Rotation takes effect immediately. Blue's measurement layer detects rotation only after a 1-turn observation lag, and re-instrumenting analytics to the new vector costs 1 additional turn plus an action point. Net: Red always enjoys a minimum 2-turn shadow in which yield extraction is unobserved or uncontested. Adjudicators must not allow Blue "we noticed immediately" moves absent a pre-built real-time monitoring capability on the ledger.

1.3 Speed-mismatch resolution. When Red and Blue plays collide mid-flight (Red rotates while Blue is mid-deployment), resolve in Red's favor unless Blue's play was declared ≥2 turns prior AND survived its stall rolls. Ties break to the side with the shorter remaining clock.

2. BLUE STALL MECHANICS (PILOT PURGATORY)

At each Blue deployment milestone (pilot → limited production → enterprise), roll d20:
- Fail threshold 11 (53% baseline stall) for safety-net/rural; 9 for regional systems; 7 for AMC/large integrated systems.
- Modifiers: −2 if CFO sponsorship logged on ledger; −2 if vendor bears implementation risk; +3 if play requires EHR-vendor cooperation; +2 if clinician committee sign-off pending; +2 if Blue took a margin hit >100bps in the prior two turns (capital freeze).
- Stall result: clock pauses 1–2 turns (d3−1). Two consecutive stalls = pilot death; play is lost, half the action points refunded.

3. BLUE'S SLOW COUNTERVAILING POWERS (MILESTONE LEDGER)

Litigation: file turn T; discovery milestone T+3; ruling/settlement T+5–6. Effects retroactive to filing only on bad-faith findings. Legislation/regulation: coalition evidence package T+2; statute or CMS rule T+4–6; Red gets one compliance-reengineering turn before enforcement bites. Coalition formation (multi-system data pooling, joint contracting): T+2–3 to bind; defection roll each turn until bound. Employer channel (self-funded plaintiffs, direct contracting): T+3–4; strongest effect, hardest milestone gates. These are the ONLY instruments that alter Red's structural behavior; operational plays merely contest yield.

4. RED COOPERATION FLIP CONDITIONS

Red signs cooperative instruments (gold-carding, real-time adjudication compacts, shared clinical-criteria APIs) only when its own ledger shows at least one of: (a) administrative cost of friction exceeds recovered yield on a line of business (appeals-overturn rate >60% sustained 2 turns while Blue autonomous-appeals volume triples cost per denial); (b) regulatory exposure priced in — active litigation past discovery plus pending statute creates expected penalty > 2x friction yield; (c) network adequacy or employer-channel threat — an ASO client >5% of book credibly moves to direct contracting; (d) MA Stars/marketplace reputation events with quantified enrollment impact. Adjudicators reject "goodwill" cooperation. Cooperation is scoped: Red gold-cards the contested vector and rotates yield elsewhere unless the instrument covers rotation explicitly.

5. ADJUDICATION EXAMPLES

Ex. 1 — Rotation beats deployment. T3: Blue regional system declares denial-prediction AI (clock 6). T4: Red rotates from front-end edits to post-pay downcoding of E/M levels. Ruling: Blue's model, trained on edit-era data, deploys T9 already mistargeted; effectiveness halved until 1-turn re-instrumentation; Red banks 2 turns of uncontested downcode yield.

Ex. 2 — Stall cascade. T5: Safety-net Blue's autonomous appeals pilot hits milestone; d20=13, stall (+2 clinician sign-off pending). T6: second roll fails; pilot death, capability lost, Red faces zero appeal-cost pressure — flip condition (a) recedes.

Ex. 3 — Cooperation flip. T7: AMC Blue's litigation reaches discovery (filed T4) revealing pend-algorithm documentation; T8 state legislature milestone hits; Blue's appeals AI (survived stalls, live T6) drives overturns to 65% for two turns. Condition (a)+(b) both satisfied. Ruling: Red signs gold-carding for imaging prior-auth at T9 — and legally rotates friction to pharmacy benefit, which the compact does not cover. Blue must re-instrument; new 2-turn shadow begins.
