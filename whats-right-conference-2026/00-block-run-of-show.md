# What's Right In Healthcare — 2-Hour AI & Automation Block
## Master run-of-show and build-out index

**The block's single argument, stated once:**
Buy-in is done (80% adoption activity) but readiness isn't (7% "very prepared"). These two hours close that gap at three altitudes: the **organization** (2030 operating model), the **market** (payer AI arms race strategy), and the **leader** (your personal practice as culture strategy).
