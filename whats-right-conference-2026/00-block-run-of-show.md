# What's Right In Healthcare — 2-Hour AI & Automation Block
## Master run-of-show and build-out index

**Know your room first.** The interactive middle segment has two builds, and the right one depends on who is actually in the seats:
- **Operational SVPs/VPs** (denials, AR, patient access, coding, CDI) → **Operating-Model & Workforce Lab** (`08`). Hands-on, grounded in their own pod and roster. **This is the default for the "What's Right" audience** (operators at heart).
- **CFOs / payer-strategy leaders** → **Arms Race War Game** (`01`). Game-theoretic, payer-strategy, capital-allocation.
- **Mixed room** → run the Lab (`08`) as Segment 2 and use the war game's 10-minute compression as a bridge, or vice-versa.

**The block's single argument, stated once:**
Buy-in is done (80% adoption activity) but readiness isn't (7% "very prepared"). These two hours close that gap at three altitudes: the **organization** (2030 operating model), the **people** (workforce transition), and the **leader** (your personal practice as culture strategy).
